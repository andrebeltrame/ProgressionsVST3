PROGRESSIONS 1.0.0
Nowhr Dynamics

Drop in a MIDI clip, or type a chord progression, and Progressions writes the
parts that go with it: pads, melodies, counter melodies, basslines, arps and
plucks. Point it at your own MIDI collection and it learns how you write.

This folder holds one plug-in that works on both macOS and Windows.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
INSTALL - macOS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Copy "Progressions.vst3" into:

       ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/

   The Library folder is hidden. In Finder, open the "Go" menu, hold the
   Option key, and "Library" appears in the list.

2. If you downloaded this from the internet, run this once in Terminal:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Progressions.vst3

   Without it macOS refuses to load the plug-in and your DAW skips it
   silently - no error, it just never shows up. The plug-in is not yet
   registered with Apple, which is the only reason this step exists.

3. Restart your DAW and rescan your plug-ins.
   In Ableton Live: Preferences -> Plug-Ins, hold Option and click Rescan.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
INSTALL - Windows
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. BEFORE extracting the .zip: right-click it, choose Properties, tick
   "Unblock" at the bottom, and click OK. Then extract.

   Windows marks anything downloaded, the mark passes to the extracted
   files, and a marked plug-in is refused without a word. If you already
   extracted, delete the result, unblock the .zip and extract again.

2. Copy the "Progressions.vst3" folder into:

       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\

   It will ask for administrator permission. If your DAW is set to scan a
   different VST3 folder, copy it there instead.

3. Restart your DAW and rescan your plug-ins.
   In Ableton Live: Preferences -> Plug-Ins, make sure "Use VST3 Plug-In
   System Folders" is On, then hold Alt and click Rescan.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
USING IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Progressions is an INSTRUMENT, not an audio effect - look under Instruments
in your plug-in browser.

Load a MIDI clip, type a progression like "Am | F | C | G", or pick one of
the 36 presets. Choose a part, and drag the result into your DAW with
"Drag MIDI to your DAW", or save it with "Save .mid".

To make it write with your own habits, click "Learn from my library..." and
point it at a folder of MIDI. It reads the files, keeps statistics - never
phrases - and writes with your vocabulary from then on. Your collection
never leaves your machine, and no clip of yours can be reconstructed from
what it keeps.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
PORTUGUES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

macOS: copie "Progressions.vst3" para ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ (a pasta
Biblioteca e oculta: no Finder, menu Ir, segure Option). Se baixou da
internet, rode uma vez no Terminal:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Progressions.vst3

Sem isso o macOS recusa o plugin e o DAW o ignora em silencio.

Windows: ANTES de extrair, botao direito no .zip, Propriedades, marque
"Desbloquear". So entao extraia e copie a pasta "Progressions.vst3" para
C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\.

Nos dois casos: reinicie o DAW e faca um rescan de plugins. No Ableton Live,
segure Option (Mac) ou Alt (Windows) ao clicar em Rescan. O Progressions
aparece em Instrumentos, nao em efeitos.
