Progressions — Nowhr Dynamics

Drop in a MIDI clip, or type a progression, and Progressions writes the parts that go with it: pads, melodies, counter melodies, basslines, arps and plucks. Point it at your own MIDI collection and it learns how you write.

This installs the VST3 into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Nowhr Dynamics/. Restart your DAW and rescan your plug-ins afterwards.
